# 📜 PASO Code of Conduct

## Welcome

Thank you for contributing to PASO.

To maintain a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative environment,
all contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to
follow this Code of Conduct.

By participating in this project, you agree to uphold the standards
outlined below.

---

# 🤝 Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation
in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of:

- age
- body size
- disability
- ethnicity
- gender identity and expression
- level of experience
- education
- socio-economic status
- nationality
- personal appearance
- race
- religion
- sexual identity and orientation

We are committed to creating an open, inclusive, and respectful environment
for everyone involved in the project.

---

# 🌟 Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy and kindness toward other community members
- Helping create a collaborative and supportive atmosphere

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others’ private information without explicit permission
- Personal attacks or discriminatory behavior
- Spam, intimidation, or disruptive conduct
- Any behavior that could reasonably be considered inappropriate
  in a professional environment

---

# 🛡 Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards
of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response
to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful
to the community.

---

# 🌐 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies across all PASO community spaces, including:

- GitHub repository
- Issues and pull requests
- Discussions and project communication channels
- Community interactions related to PASO
- Any official project spaces managed by contributors or maintainers

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is representing
the project or its community in public spaces.

---

# ⚖ Enforcement Actions

Depending on the severity of violations, project maintainers may:

- Issue warnings
- Remove inappropriate comments or content
- Temporarily restrict participation
- Reject contributions or pull requests
- Permanently ban contributors from the community

Enforcement decisions will aim to protect the health, safety, and integrity
of the PASO community.
